If you have a slide that other churches should see, you can submit it. An
administrator reviews it before it becomes visible to anyone.

## How to submit

1. Open **Submit a Slide** from the menu.
2. Choose your image (or video) and give the slide a **title** that says what it is for.
3. Optionally, add **notes for administrators**: what the slide is for, or when it should run.
4. Choose **Submit for Review**.

You'll see a confirmation once the upload finishes. Large files upload in parts,
so keep the page open until it says the slide was submitted.

## What happens next

A submitted slide waits in the review queue. An administrator then either
approves it, which publishes it, or rejects it. Nothing is shown publicly while
it is waiting. See [Reviewing submissions](help:reviewing-submissions) for what
the administrator sees.

> Slides are shown full screen on a TV, so a 16:9 picture (for example
> 1920 × 1080) looks best. The site can enlarge smaller images for you when you
> upload them.
