The bell at the top of every page shows how many unread notifications you
have. Click it to see the newest ones, or choose **View all notifications**
for your full inbox.

## What you are notified about

What you receive depends on your role:

- **Everyone:** when a slide you submitted is approved or not approved.
- **Church leaders and editors:**
  - a new global slide is available;
  - a nearby church shared a slide;
  - a new promo show is available;
  - someone accepted your invitation to the team.

  Slide notices list each church you manage and the shows the slide was
  added to automatically, or say it wasn't added, so you know whether to add
  it yourself.
- **Site admins:**
  - slides waiting for review;
  - slides shared with nearby churches;
  - global slides a contributor published.
- **Optional:** when a scheduled slide starts showing or a slide expires.
  These are off by email by default.

## Inbox and archive

Each notification is yours alone: clearing it doesn't affect anyone else.

- Click a notification to open what it is about. This also marks it read.
- **Clear** (×) moves a notification to the **Archive**. **Clear all** does
  this for the whole inbox, or for one category while a category filter is
  selected.
- Archived notifications can be restored. They are deleted automatically
  after a while (90 days unless your site admin changed it).

## Email

On your **Profile** page, under **Notification settings**, you can choose
for each category:

- whether it shows in your inbox;
- whether it is emailed: **No email**, **Daily digest** (one summary email a
  day) or **Right away**.

The daily digest leaves out notifications you have already read in the app.
Every email has a link to stop that kind of email.

If you never get any email, your site admin may not have turned notification
email on yet.
