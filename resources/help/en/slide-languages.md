A slide can be tagged with a language, such as English or Spanish.

- A slide **with** a language tag is shown to people using that language.
- A slide **with no** language tag is shown to everyone.

The site uses your browser's language by default. On the Announcements page you
can choose a different language to look at; this only changes what you see.

## For people who add slides

When you upload or edit a slide, choose its language, or choose **No specific
language (visible in all)** for slides that work for everyone, such as a picture
with no words.

## For screens

Each paired Announcer device also has a language setting, which filters the
slides that sync to it. See [Pairing a Slide Announcer](help:pairing-a-device).
