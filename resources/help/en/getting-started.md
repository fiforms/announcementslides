This site collects announcement slides for churches. A conference or church
publishes a slide once, and any church that follows it can show it on a screen
or download it, so nobody has to forward files by email.

## Finding slides

The **Announcements** page lists the current slides. Slides come from three places:

- **Everyone:** slides published for all churches.
- **Your churches:** slides a church you belong to has published just for its own members.
- **Nearby churches:** slides other churches chose to share with neighbours, if you turn on *include nearby*.

If you belong to more than one church, use the church menu at the top to switch
between them, or choose **Global View** to see only the slides for everyone.

## Showing or downloading a slide

Open a slide to see it full size. You can download a single slide, or select
several and download them together as a ZIP file or a PowerPoint. Slides that
have expired move to the church's archive and stop appearing in the main list.

## Language

Slides can be tagged with a language. You see the slides tagged for your
language plus every slide with no language. See [Slide languages](help:slide-languages).

## Need an account?

You can browse public slides without signing in. Sign in to follow a church,
submit a slide for review ([Submitting a slide](help:submitting-a-slide)), or
manage your church's slides.
