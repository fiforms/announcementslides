Every slide has a status, and a few simple rules decide whether people see it.

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Draft | Saved but not yet sent for anyone to see. |
| Pending | Submitted by a viewer and waiting for review. |
| Published | Approved and available, subject to its dates. |
| Rejected | Reviewed and turned down. |

## Dates decide the rest

A published slide only appears **between its publish date and its expiry date**.
Before the publish date it is *scheduled*; after the expiry date it is
*archived*. There is no background job: these are worked out every time a page
loads, so you never have to wait for anything to switch over.

## Reviewing

Open **Admin → Slides** to find slides waiting for review. Approve a slide to
publish it, or reject it. The notes the submitter wrote are shown with it.
You can also reorder published slides and archive them early.

## Slides for one church

A slide attached to a church is only seen by that church's members; a slide
with no church is global and public.
